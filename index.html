Using your selected set. Enter local card numbers separated by commas, spaces, or new lines (e.g. 4, 12, 65, 101). Review the results before adding.

📸 Identify from a photo (beta)

Tip: photograph one card straight-on, with the printed collector number near the bottom clear and in focus. The first scan downloads an OCR library.

Keep your collection safe

Your Team Reeves collection is saved locally in this browser—not automatically synced to other devices. Download a backup after a big scanning session.

Import replaces local saved cards and showcase picks after confirmation. Backups also work with the earlier binder version; cards you already marked will carry over when you use this version in the same browser.

Unofficial fan project. Images and catalog data via TCGdex; not associated with The Pokémon Company. Card data and art require internet. OCR uses Tesseract.js downloaded on demand; photos are not intentionally uploaded to this app's server. Browser storage can be lost if website data is cleared. Prices below are manually entered estimates, not live market quotes. This site does not synchronize collections online.
