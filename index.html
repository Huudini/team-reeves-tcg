📷 Scan a 3 × 3 page of cards

Photograph up to nine upright cards in an evenly spaced 3-by-3 grid. Use Mixed sets for assorted cards, or Single set for faster, more focused matching. The scanner reads printed card names and numbers, then suggests catalog matches for you to confirm. It cannot guarantee identification from artwork alone.

Fastest ways to add cards

For stacks from one expansion, using card numbers is fastest. For one-offs, photograph the bottom of the card or search in the binder.

⚡ Bulk add card numbers

Choose any set, then enter local collector numbers separated by commas, spaces, or new lines (e.g. 4, 12, 65, 101). Review matches before adding to your collection.

📸 Identify from a photo (beta)

Photo scanning runs OCR in your browser. It reads the printed card number and suggests a match within the selected set. This is not guaranteed image recognition; always confirm the card before adding it.
Tip: photograph one card straight-on, with the printed collector number near the bottom clear and in focus. The first scan downloads an OCR library.

Keep your collection safe

Export a JSON backup whenever you make major changes. With family cloud enabled, your owned cards, favorites, and manual values synchronize across approved devices.

Import replaces local saved cards and showcase picks after confirmation. Backups also work with the earlier binder version; cards you already marked will carry over when you use this version in the same browser.

Unofficial fan project. Images and catalog data via TCGdex; not associated with The Pokémon Company. Card data and art require internet. OCR uses Tesseract.js downloaded on demand; photos are not intentionally uploaded to this app's server. Browser storage can be lost if website data is cleared. Prices are sourced from available TCGdex TCGplayer market quotes (USD), or labeled manual estimates. Quotes may be missing, outdated, or inaccurate. Prices do not account for condition or grading.
