📷 Scan a 3 × 3 page of cards

Photograph up to nine upright cards in an evenly spaced 3-by-3 grid. Use Mixed sets for assorted cards, or Single set for faster, more focused matching. The scanner reads printed card names and numbers, then suggests catalog matches for you to confirm. It cannot guarantee identification from artwork alone.
